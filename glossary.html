Glossary

The words PHSMS uses.

Plain-language meanings for the Philippine school terms you will see in PHSMS.

Advisory section
The section a teacher is the adviser of. Teachers take daily attendance for their advisory section.
DepEd
The Department of Education. PHSMS’s default grading scheme is modelled on DepEd’s approach, and your school can adjust it.
DepEd school ID
The identifier DepEd assigns to a school. It is stored on the school profile.
Form 137
A learner’s permanent academic record. PHSMS stores it as a student document type, alongside birth certificates, report cards and good moral certificates.
Learning area
A subject or group of subjects. PHSMS groups subjects into Languages, AP and EsP, Science and Math, MAPEH, EPP and TLE, and the senior high groups.
MAPEH, EPP, TLE
Subject groupings used in basic education: Music, Arts, Physical Education and Health; Edukasyong Pantahanan at Pangkabuhayan; and Technology and Livelihood Education.
AP and EsP
Araling Panlipunan (social studies) and Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao (values education).
Quarter
One of the four grading periods in the school year. Grades are entered, approved and published per quarter.
Senior high strand
A specialised track in grades 11 and 12. PHSMS lets a school set up the strands it offers.
Transmutation
Converting a raw percentage into the reported grade using a table. PHSMS supports the DepEd 2015 table, or none.
Written Work, Performance Task, Quarterly Assessment
The three components of a grade in the DepEd-style scheme, each with its own weight that varies by subject group.
Semestral
Paid twice a year. One of the payment plans a fee schedule can offer, with annual, quarterly and monthly.
Promotion and retention
Whether a learner moves up to the next grade level or repeats the year. The registrar records each decision, with any exceptions.
Data Privacy Act
The Philippine law on handling personal information. PHSMS shows a notice on the public application form and records its version.
Rollover
Moving the school from one school year to the next. PHSMS lets the registrar plan it, review it and then apply it.
Sensitive records
Health, guidance and safeguarding records. They are kept separate, left out of exports and dashboards, and every access is logged.
