Built for how Philippine schools work

Three things schools tell us matter most.

Kinder to Grade 12, with senior high

Four school stages (Kinder, Elementary, Junior High, Senior High), senior high strands, and subject groups such as Languages, AP and EsP, Science and Math, MAPEH, EPP and TLE.

Billing the way schools bill

Tuition, miscellaneous fees and books on annual, semestral, quarterly or monthly plans, with sibling discounts, scholarships and waivers.

Privacy built into admissions

The public application form shows a Data Privacy Act notice, and the version of the notice is recorded with each application.

Grades parents can trust

A DepEd-style gradebook where grades move from draft to approved to published. Parents and students only ever see published grades.

A cashier’s desk with a clean trail

Pesos, installment plans, sequential receipts and duplicate protection. Mistakes are reversed with a reason, never deleted.

Families kept in the loop

Announcements aimed at one grade or one family, required notices parents must acknowledge, and a portal that shows only what has been released.

Trust & security

Each school’s data stays its own.

PHSMS serves many schools from one platform, and every record is tied to its school. One school can never see another’s data.

Access checked on every record

Roles decide what you can do. A second check decides whether you may touch this particular student, invoice or grade.

An audit trail you can read

Every change records who did it and when, and owners and auditors can review the school’s audit log.

Sensitive records, extra care

Health, guidance and safeguarding records are kept apart, left out of exports, and every access is logged.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is PHSMS only for one school?

No. PHSMS is multi-tenant: many schools run on one platform, each with its own data, logo and brand colour. A school can never see another school’s information.

Can we bring our existing student records?

Yes. Upload CSV or Excel files for students, guardians, enrolments, staff, subjects, sections and fees. Every row is validated, you get an error report for anything that needs fixing, and a whole import can be rolled back.

Can parents pay online?

Not yet. Today the cashier records payments, and parents see their statements and receipts in the portal. Online payment is planned for a later release.

Is two-factor sign-in or single sign-on available?

Not yet. It is planned for a later release. Sessions use secure cookies, passwords are hashed, and sign-in attempts are rate limited.
