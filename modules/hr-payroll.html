HR & payroll

Keep staff records, leave and pay in one place that only the right people can see, with payroll that cannot be approved by the person who prepared it.

Staff recordsLeave & attendancePayroll
Staff records

Everything about a staff member, in one profile.

Employment, contracts and credentials, with reminders before anything lapses.

What is included

  • Staff profiles with employment details and contracts
  • Credentials and licences with expiry tracking
  • Staff documents stored securely
  • Compensation changes are logged
Leave & attendance

Leave that also protects the timetable.

Approved leave feeds the timetable, so the school sees cover needs before the day starts.

What is included

  • Leave types, balances, requests and approvals
  • Approved leave shows cover needs for the timetable and substitute warnings
  • Staff attendance
  • Staff can view their own leave and request time off

Examples of leave types (your school sets its own)

  • Vacation
  • Sick
  • Emergency
  • Maternity
  • Paternity
  • Leave without pay
Payroll

Payroll with two sets of eyes.

Payroll access is kept separate from staff management, and runs are approved by a different person.

What is included

  • Payroll periods, components, runs and payslips
  • A run must be approved by someone other than the person who prepared it
  • Payroll access separate from general staff management
  • Staff view their own payslips
Who uses it

Built for the people who do the work.

  • HR officer
  • School owner
  • Principal
  • Every staff member (own leave and payslips)

See what every role can do

Good to know: not included yet

  • Statutory tax and contribution calculations.
  • Payslip PDF generation. Payslips are printed from the browser.
