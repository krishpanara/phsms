Academic structure

Your school year, set up once and reused.

Grade levels from Kinder to Grade 12, senior high strands, sections and the teachers who run them, all tied to the active school year.

What is included

  • School years, quarters or terms, holidays and calendar events, with one active year
  • Grade levels from Kinder to Grade 12, subjects and learning areas, and senior high strands
  • Sections with adviser, room and capacity, plus a room list
  • Class offerings: a section, a subject and a teacher for each school year
  • Enrol students into sections, and keep a history of every section change

Timetable & events

Build timetables without double-booking anyone.

Lay out the school day, place classes, and let the system warn you about clashes before anything is published.

What is included

  • School-day templates with periods, breaks and rotating schedules
  • A timetable builder: section, subject, teacher, room, day and period, with effective dates
  • Conflict checks for teachers, rooms and sections before you publish
  • Publish and notify affected users, with a change log that records who changed what
  • Temporary changes and substitute teachers, with cover needs shown when leave is approved
  • Views for each teacher, section, room, student and parent
  • School events with venue, capacity, registration and guardian consent, plus room bookings with approval

Attendance

Roll call in seconds, with the reports leaders need.

Teachers mark the whole class present and then fix the exceptions. Corrections are always explained and always logged.

What is included

  • Daily attendance for the advisory section, with “all present” then exceptions
  • Present, absent, late, excused, school activity or remote
  • Corrections require a reason and are kept in the audit trail
  • Guardian email when a child is marked absent, configurable per school
  • Reports: daily summary, monthly per section, chronic absence and tardiness
Gradebook & report cards

DepEd-style grading, with an approval step.

Teachers enter scores and the system does the arithmetic. Nothing reaches a parent until it has been approved and published.

Grade release workflow

  1. Draft
  2. Submitted
  3. Approved
  4. Published
  5. Locked
  • A default DepEd-style scheme (Written Work, Performance Task, Quarterly Assessment) with weights per subject group and a transmutation table, configurable per school
  • Teachers create assessment items, enter scores in bulk, and get warnings about missing marks
  • A calculation breakdown shows how every grade was reached
  • Absent, excused and incomplete are recorded separately from a zero
  • Reopening a grade needs approval and keeps the history
  • Printable report cards per student per year, saved as versioned snapshots

Parents and students only ever see published grades.
