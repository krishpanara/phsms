Reports

The reports your school asks for.

Reports are permission-checked, so each person only sees what they are allowed to. Exports are audit-logged and made safe to open in a spreadsheet.

Good to know

  • Exports are CSV only. Excel and PDF exports are not available yet.
  • A single export is capped at 10,000 rows.
  • Auditors can view reports but cannot download exports.
  • Every export is written to the audit log.
  • Roles shown are the defaults, and your school’s access settings still apply.
