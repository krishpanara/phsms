Roles

The right screen for every person.

Fifteen built-in roles. Each person gets a view built for their work, and sees only the records they are allowed to, down to the individual student.

Leadership

School owner

The big picture: enrolment, attendance and collections. Approves discounts and controls school-wide settings and data exports.

Principal

Oversees academics and operations, approves grades before release, and approves discounts.

School admin

Sets up the school, manages staff accounts and roles, and keeps calendars in order.

Auditor

Read-only access to everything in the school, with no ability to change a record.

School office

Registrar

Admissions, student records, document requests, promotion decisions and school-year rollover.

Finance

Fee schedules, invoices, payments, receipts, statements and balances.

HR officer

Staff records, leave, staff attendance and payroll.

Librarian

Catalogue, circulation, reservations and fines.

Transport coordinator

Vehicles, routes, assignments, and pickup and drop-off events.

Classroom & care

Teacher

Attendance for their advisory section, the gradebook, their timetable and messages from parents.

School nurse

Health records, clinic visits and medication logs, kept confidential and access-logged.

Guidance counselor

Guidance cases and confidential notes, kept apart from the student record.

Families

Parent

Everything about their children in one portal: attendance, published grades, statements, announcements and messages.

Student

Their own attendance, published grades, timetable and announcements.

Platform

Super admin

Runs the platform: creates and suspends schools, sets each school’s logo and brand colour, and invites the first owner.

Access

Roles are only half of it.

Knowing someone’s role is not enough. PHSMS also checks that they may see this particular record, so a teacher sees their own sections, and a parent sees only their own children.

How access works

  • Everything is denied unless a role is explicitly allowed
  • Every record belongs to one school and is never visible to another
  • Auditors can read everything in their school but change nothing
  • Health, guidance and safeguarding records have their own, stricter access
  • Hiding a button is never treated as security. The server checks every request
