Platform

Set up and branded for your school.

Getting a new school ready does not need a consultant, and every school looks like its own.

See the setup steps

What is included

  • Guided setup with a progress checklist
  • School profile with contact details, address, DepEd school ID, timezone and campuses
  • School years, quarters, holidays and the school calendar
  • Staff invites by email, role assignment, deactivation and reactivation
  • Your logo and brand colour in your school’s sidebar
  • A school audit log viewer for owners and auditors
  • A platform console for creating, suspending and reactivating schools
Shared foundations

What every module has in common.

One record, everywhere

A student entered once is the same student in attendance, grades, invoices and the parent portal.

Role-based access

Fifteen roles, each opening to a view built for their work, with per-record checks behind it.

Audit trail

Changes are recorded with who and when, so questions can be answered later.

Exports with care

Reports and exports are permission-checked and logged. Sensitive data is left out.

Built for the Philippines

DepEd-style grading, Kinder to Grade 12 with senior high strands, and billing in pesos.

Made for phones too

Responsive screens, so teachers and parents can use it from whatever device they have.
