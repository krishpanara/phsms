Trust & security

Built so each school’s data stays its own.

Schools hold some of the most private information there is. PHSMS is designed so that it is separated, access is checked every time, and what happens to it can be seen.

SeparationAccess controlAudit trailSensitive recordsAccounts & sessionsFiles & exportsApprovals
Separation

Each school’s data stays its own.

PHSMS is built for many schools on one platform, and every record belongs to exactly one school.

In practice

  • Every record is tied to its school, and requests are scoped to the signed-in user’s school
  • One school can never read or change another school’s information
  • One email address belongs to one school account
Access control

Checked on every request, and every record.

The server decides what you can do. Hiding something in the screen is never the only protection.

In practice

  • Role-based access on every endpoint, denied unless explicitly allowed
  • A second check that you may access this specific student, invoice or grade
  • A read-only auditor role that cannot change records
  • Parents see only their own children, and only released information
Audit trail

See who did what, and when.

Changes are recorded so questions about them can be answered later.

In practice

  • Changes to records are written to an audit log with the person and the time
  • A school audit log viewer for owners and auditors, and a platform audit log for the platform team
  • Attendance corrections, grade reopening and payment reversals all require a reason
  • Data exports and report exports are audit-logged
Sensitive records

Extra care for the most private information.

Health, guidance and safeguarding records are the most sensitive a school holds.

In practice

  • Kept apart from the everyday student record
  • Left out of exports, dashboards and audit-log views
  • Every access is written to a sensitive access log, with a review page
  • Parents see outcome notices only, never staff notes
Accounts & sessions

Safe by default.

The basics, done carefully.

In practice

  • Passwords are stored hashed, never in readable form
  • Sessions use secure, httpOnly cookies that scripts on the page cannot read
  • Sign-in and public endpoints are rate limited
  • Staff accounts are invited by email, and can be deactivated at any time
Files & exports

Uploads and exports, handled with care.

Documents and downloads are where private information most often leaves a system.

In practice

  • Uploads are checked for file type and size
  • Documents open through short-lived secure links, not public web addresses
  • CSV exports are made safe to open in spreadsheet programs, and each export is capped in size
  • Every export is permission-checked and audit-logged
  • Auditors can read reports but cannot download exports
  • Student documents and staff documents are stored for separate purposes
Approvals

Two sets of eyes where it matters.

Money and pay need a second person.

In practice

  • Payroll runs are approved by someone other than the person who prepared them
  • Discounts and scholarships need an approver and a stated reason
  • Inventory write-offs need approval
  • Payments are never deleted, only reversed with a reason, so the history stays complete

Good to know: not available yet

  • Two-factor sign-in and single sign-on are planned for a later release.
  • If your school has specific security or data-privacy questions, contact us and we will answer them directly.
